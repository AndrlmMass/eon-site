Privacy policy

EON for Android. Last updated 15 November 2026.

What EON reads

From Health Connect, read-only: sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, VO₂max, steps, distance, workouts and their GPS routes, calories burned, weight and mindfulness sessions. EON uses them to work out your scores and trends, and never writes back. You can take any of this away in Health Connect at any time.

You can also add things yourself: meals, workouts, check-ins such as mood, and a profile with your birth year, sex and goals.

Where it stays

On your phone. There's no EON account and no EON server, so nobody receives your data from EON. Uninstalling the app deletes its copy. If Android's backup to your Google account is on, it may include EON's data, but never your API keys.

What leaves the phone

Only this, and only when you use the feature:

  • Scanning a barcode: the barcode goes to Open Food Facts.
  • Opening a map: the map pieces for that area are fetched from MapTiler or Esri, so they can see roughly where the map shows.
  • AI, only if you turn it on: your questions, meal descriptions and photos, the data needed to answer them and the key numbers behind the written notes go to the AI service you connect, under your own account. EON asks before it sends anything the first time. With AI off, nothing goes there.

How Health Connect data is used

EON uses data from Health Connect only to show you your own scores, trends and notes in the app. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone, except with the AI service you connect yourself, and only while AI is on.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete everything clears your meals, workouts, check-ins, profile, settings and API keys from the phone, and EON starts over. Uninstalling does the same. Health Connect keeps its own data, which you manage in Health Connect.

Not medical advice

EON's numbers are estimates for wellness. They aren't a diagnosis or medical advice, and EON isn't a medical device. See a doctor about symptoms or anything that worries you.

Who EON is for

EON is for adults, 18 and over.
